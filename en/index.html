AboutProfile

I'm Seongmin Lee.
I'm aiming to become a sound designer, focused on sound effects (SFX), who brings game worlds to life through sound.

I'm from Korea. I studied vocals at an arts high school, then performed live and learned composition after graduating. After my military service, I kept composing mainly on commission from 2020, and moved to Japan in 2026.

At HAL Tokyo I discovered sound design. I was drawn to how a single sound can change the atmosphere of a scene and pull players into the story. I'm now studying with a focus on sound effects, with the goal of becoming a game sound designer.
